Passive Pals
============

Pals outside your control stop attacking you on sight, stop fleeing when you
come near, and always fight back when actually attacked, regardless of level
difference. Humans keep their vanilla behavior by default. Your own pals and
base workers are untouched.

Installed through the Palworld mod loader (Options > Mod Management) with the
UE4SS Experimental (Palworld) Workshop item as its dependency.

Configuration: edit Scripts\settings.lua inside the installed mod folder and
save it. The change applies within a couple of seconds while you play; no
restart. The file documents everything: behavior profiles, assignment
by kind of character, per-preset and per-species assignment, the petting
switch, logging, and the full reaction glossary.
